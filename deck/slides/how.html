How it works

AI reads the documents. Rules set the number.

1

Capture

A monthly log records fuel used and output. Gemini reads certificates, invoices and fuel bills. Every figure links to its source.

2

Match

Certificates give the mill and heat. Invoices give the tonnes. Joined by heat number; conflicts are flagged.

3

Calculate

Versioned rules add furnace fuel to purchased steel. Verified mills use actual values; others use defaults.

4

Hand off

A verifier pack: every figure with its source, the open gaps, and an EU supplier template.

5

Demo: load six certificates, an invoice, three fuel bills and a production record. Click any figure to see its source. Point out the certificate that only says “46 coils”: the tonnes come from the trader’s invoice for the same heat.
