The insight

A fastener’s carbon number is mostly its steel

0.19 own furnaces

4.20 purchased steel, mostly on defaults

tCO2 per tonne of bolts, demo plant of 1,000 t, reporting year 2027. Total 4.39. The steel default is India wire rod at 4.3 before the mark-up, from a secondary source. Mill values are demo data.

Actual steel values count only if they come from a verified report on the mill that made the steel. So a fastener maker’s number is only as good as its wire-rod supplier’s data.

3

This is why “AI that collects plant data” is not enough. The plant’s own data is under 5% of the number. The rest sits with the mill.
