The problem

EU importers will price Indian bolts on penalty defaults

30 Sep 2027

First annual CBAM declaration. Fasteners (CN 7318) are already in scope.

+10 → 30%

Penalty mark-up on EU default values: 10% in 2026, 20% in 2027, 30% from 2028.

2.5 → 100%

Share of the CBAM charge that is payable, from 2026 to 2034.

Why defaults win today: plants keep invoices, not CBAM data, and the experts who could fix that are scarce. Fuel used, monthly output and a monitoring plan are not recorded. And most of the number is not theirs to record.

2

Two gaps. One: plants record purchases, not usage, and have no monitoring plan, so a verifier rejects the report. Two: most of the number is the mill’s, next slide. EU fastener distributors already expect importers to fall back on defaults. The deadline is real and close. Without verified data, the importer pays on defaults plus a penalty, and the penalty grows. That makes Indian suppliers more expensive to buy from. Source for the EFDA view: EFDA supplier guide and Eurometal coverage.
