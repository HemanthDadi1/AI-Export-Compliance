AI compliance expert for Indian exporters

A clerk uploads documents and logs fuel and output. AI reads and matches them. Rules do the calculation. This is a static demo of the result.

Made-up data. Plants, mills and mill values are invented. The steel default (4.3 tCO2/t before the mark-up) comes from a secondary source and is not confirmed. Not an EU-approved calculation. The numbers come from the real engine in this repo. The app itself runs locally: see the repository.
