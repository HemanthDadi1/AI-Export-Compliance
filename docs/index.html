Questions

Is the report approved by the EU?

No. It is a draft that an accredited verifier checks and signs. We prepare the data, the sources and the gaps so the verifier spends less time on it.

Does AI decide our emissions number?

No. AI only reads the documents. You check and confirm every value, and fixed, versioned rules do every calculation.

What does "confirm" mean?

Each uploaded file shows the values we read next to the line they came from. You correct anything wrong and confirm. Corrections are marked in the report, so a verifier can see them.

Which other rules are coming?

Scope 1–3 supplier emissions for buyers, India's carbon market (CCTS), and California SB 253. They are on the roadmap, not built. Join the waitlist below to hear first.

Where is my data kept?

In the free demo, files stay in your browser. The hosted app is designed to run on Google Cloud in Mumbai so plant data stays in India.
