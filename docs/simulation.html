Network simulation: how fast does verifying mills help every plant?

Verify a mill once and every plant that buys its steel moves from the EU default to the mill's actual value. This simulation shows how fast that spreads.

Our assumptions, not data. The plants, mills and mill values are invented. The chance a mill joins is the biggest unknown and has not been tested with any real mill. The emissions arithmetic is the real engine in this repo. See the demo or the code.
